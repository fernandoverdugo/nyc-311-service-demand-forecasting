Operational interpretation

Citywide daily demand is comparatively stable: the simple 28-day rolling mean outperformed the more complex statistical and machine-learning benchmarks on mean out-of-time WAPE. Category-level demand is much less uniform, so service lines should be forecast using models matched to their behavior.

Staffing assumes 45 requests per FTE per day at 85.0% target utilization. This is an illustrative scenario, not an NYC staffing standard.

Decision takeaway

Use simple models where they work. Aggregate 311 demand is forecastable with a recent-level baseline, while volatile categories benefit from service-specific seasonal or lag/calendar models. Forecast uncertainty should be translated into planning ranges rather than a single staffing number.

Source: NYC Open Data, 311 Service Requests from 2020 to Present (dataset erm2-nwe9). Forecast benchmark uses a fixed historical analysis window for reproducibility.
